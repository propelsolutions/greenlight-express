GREENLIGHT EXPRESS LLC – SMS TERMS & CONDITIONS
Effective Date: July 3, 2026

By opting into text messaging from GREENLIGHT EXPRESS LLC via our website contact form, you agree to the following terms and conditions regarding our conversational and operational SMS communications.

1. Program Description & Use Case

GREENLIGHT EXPRESS LLC utilizes SMS strictly for two-way, human-to-human conversational and transactional messages between our logistics coordinators and external freight brokers or clients. Text messages are used exclusively to coordinate active freight contracts, provide lane updates, confirm pickup/delivery schedules, and respond directly to inquiries you initiate. We do not send marketing, promotional, or automated broadcast messages.

2. Standard Messaging Disclosures

  • Message and Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier depending on your mobile plan.
  • Frequency: Text messaging frequency varies and is entirely dependent on your active logistics runs, contract coordination, and service inquiries.

3. How to Opt-Out (Unsubscribe)

You can completely cancel and stop receiving text messages from us at any time. Simply reply STOP to any text message we send you. After texting STOP, you will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent.

4. How to Get Help

If you experience any issues or need assistance, simply reply HELP to any message, or call/text our main office directly at (763) 290-0939.
